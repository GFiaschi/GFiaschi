# Hi there, I'm Gabriele Fiaschi 👋

I'm a researcher bridging theoretical physics, complex systems and artificial intelligence. Currently, I am a PhD Student in the *Brain, Mind and Computer Science* program at **Università degli Studi di Padova** & **Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK)**. 

My work focuses on translating complex biological processes into mathematical frameworks to build advanced, bio-inspired AI models.

### 🎓 Academic Background
- **Ph.D. in Computer Science** (Societal Challenges & Innovation) - *UniPD & FBK* (Ongoing)
- **M.Sc. in Physics of Complex Systems** - *Università degli Studi di Padova*
- **B.Sc. in Physics** - *Università degli Studi di Padova*

### 🧠 Research Focus
- **Spiking Neural Networks:** Developing bio-inspired, multi-scale architectures for complex time-series modeling.
- **Neuroimaging & fMRI:** Modeling whole-brain functional connectivity and predicting brain dynamics from empirical data.
- **Continual Learning:** Exploring associative memory models and advanced training paradigms to mitigate catastrophic forgetting in artificial neural networks.
- **HPC Computations:** Designing and executing rigorous benchmark-driven experiments on SLURM clusters.
